# Which criteria could pick more CS2 winners?

Issue #56 · Sources and archive checked 2026-11-26. **Test round differential first.** A 13–3 and a 13–11 win currently supply the same map outcome. The archive preserves both scores, so this comparison needs neither a new source nor a change to the game's scoring.

The #48 anchor picks roughly 65% of winners. On the same Matches as each variant, calibration changed accuracy by −0.09 percentage points (pp), own rating by −1.20 pp and per-map rating by −1.66 pp. All three improved log-loss. The strongest positive accuracy change among the promoted signals was the Bo1 veto simulation (+0.94 pp on 319 Matches, roughly three extra winners). Its Bo3 counterpart lost 2.92 pp on 1,850 Matches. These are training-side walk-forward observations. The Singapore Holdout is pending. ## Five Candidate Signals in build order

Expected effects below are hypotheses relative to the anchor. The cited estimates for existing signals give scale. None of the sources measures the incremental accuracy of these five proposals against KallBet. Each can be tested offline at zero acquisition cost.

| Priority and criterion | What to compare and why | Archived inputs | Expected effect on correct Predictions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Opponent-adjusted round differential | Update team strength from the difference between achieved and expected round share. Compare with an otherwise identical win/loss rating. Cap blowouts and treat overtime separately. Margin-weighted Elo has been evaluated in other sports, and CS:GO work identifies map scores as pre-Match history. [Weighted Elo study](https://cris.unibo.it/retrieve/e1dcb337-ea48-7715-e053-1705fe0a6cc9/Weighted%20ELO%20rating%20predictions%20in%20tennis.pdf) · [Schmidt's CS:GO study](https://theses.liacs.nl/pdf/2019-2020-SchmidtZachary.pdf) | Prior HLTV played-map scores, or Liquipedia map totals, joined to opponents and finish times. Deduplicate across sources. | Best prospect for a small positive change, especially on Matches without a line. It can distinguish two teams with identical win/loss histories. |
| 2. Bo1 map-pool depth and habitual first ban | Estimate which maps survive each team's usual first removal, then measure how many surviving maps favour each team. Compare directly with the existing veto simulation. CS:GO research found that learning from bans improved its map-selection model. Its simulated benefit to teams choosing maps is a different quantity from forecast accuracy. [Map-selection study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.08888) | 1,970 HLTV veto histories, ordered actions, team, active pool and prior map results. Use only maps available in that pool era. | Small positive plausible on Bo1s, diluted across all Matches. The existing +0.94 pp is a comparator. Extending to Bo3 has a negative local precedent. |
| 3. Side-specific strength by map | Estimate attack (T) rounds won per T round and defence (CT) rounds won per CT round against the opponent. Shrink each rate toward the map baseline. Average over possible starting sides when unknown at Lock. A documented CS2 model uses side win rates but publishes no historical hit rate. [CS Data methodology](https://cs2data.org/en/how-it-works) | HLTV half scores joined to archived Liquipedia first-side labels by Match, map and team orientation. Use regulation halves only. | Small positive plausible on well-covered, asymmetric maps. Expect little gain overall if side differences average out. |
| 4. Ensemble of existing ratings | Average pre-Match win probabilities from VRS, own rating and per-map rating, retaining the anchor's existing treatment of the market input. Compare with the frozen Combiner. Count how often the ratings actually choose different teams. #48 found overlapping probability improvements and no second rating entered forward selection. [Selection results](../lab/research/README.md) | Archived VRS snapshots, PandaScore series results and Liquipedia prior maps. Reproduce forecasts within each Event fold. | Near zero is the leading expectation. Gains require the ensemble to correct more disagreements than it introduces. Cheap benchmark for criteria 1–3. |
| 5. Confidence from agreement | Group Predictions by pre-Match agreement between the anchor, VRS and own rating, and by distance from 50%. Where an archived market forecast and rating disagree, test each as a fallback using only earlier Events to choose the rule. Selective classification evaluates correctness alongside the fraction covered. [Selective-classification study](https://mitibm.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Fair_Selective_Classification.pdf) | Existing walk-forward ratings, dated archive forecasts and Match winners. Keep Matches without a market forecast as a separate group. | Labelling confidence alone changes overall accuracy by exactly zero. Expect higher accuracy in the high-confidence group, subject to validation. A fallback can change total Points only if it changes the chosen team. |

## Lower priorities

| Priority | Evidence, availability and cost | Expected accuracy effect |
|---|---|---|
| 6. Historical HLTV team ranking points | HLTV's own ranking combines achievements, form and LAN results. Its separate VRS display reproduces Valve standings. The snapshot supplies VRS and Match pages. A dated series of HLTV's own weekly points would require a separate historical source with publication times and team IDs. Manual public-page checks cost staff time. A complete free machine-readable history was not established. [HLTV methodology](https://www.hltv.org/news/16061/introducing-csgo-team-ranking) · [HLTV on VRS](https://www.hltv.org/news/39751/valve-ranking-and-formula-implemented-on-hltv) | Possible small gain where rankings disagree. Independent winner-picking lift remains unmeasured, and both ranks reflect past results. |
| 7. More market coverage and opening versus last available observation | #48 had a pre-cutoff observation for 777 Matches and all three sources on only 28 in one Event. All four source variants changed accuracy by zero there. Movement gave +0.13 pp on 744. Compare first and last observations available **before Hard Lock** on the same Matches. A true closing observation after Lock is ineligible. Existing history costs zero. Broader bookmaker coverage needs a dated backfill with rights and quotas checked; free coverage was not established. [Archive inventory and request ledger](../lab/research/README.md) · [Paired source tests](../lab/research/report.md) | Little evidence for a gain from switching sources on already covered Matches. Extending coverage to previously uncovered Matches could change accuracy in either direction. Measure those Matches separately. |
| 8. Map-specific player form | A published CS2 project identifies map win rates and individual statistics among its important features. The archive holds HLTV series and per-map player records, including kills, deaths, ADR and rating version, plus Liquipedia rosters. Offline cost is zero. Use the roster known at cutoff and compare like rating versions. [Authors' project and report](https://github.com/archotium/cs2-match-prediction) · [Data-source study](map-veto-player-research.md) | Near zero pending evidence on roster changes. #48 player form moved accuracy by −0.06 pp and lineup strength by −0.41 pp. |

## Archive checks and decision rule

In the frozen snapshot, 4,376 played map entries in the HLTV Match JSON have both final scores, and 4,375 have half-score text. For example, Match 2373771 records Spirit–OG on Dust2 as 13–3 with halves 10–2 and 3–1. The text supplies team order only. Archived Liquipedia wikitext contains both CT and T values for `t1firstside`. The fraction of maps with a unique, orientation-checked join remains unmeasured. Report that denominator before judging criterion 3. Exclude unplayed maps, missing finishes and ambiguous side assignments. Treat retrospective player metrics as usable only if their definition was available at the historical cutoff. [Archive schema](../../sidecar/src/adapters/hltv_research/archive.rs) · [Source notes](map-veto-player-research.md)

For each criterion, fit on completed earlier Events under #48's cutoff rules. Report correct picks gained and lost, paired Matches, coverage, net percentage-point change and a 90% whole-Event bootstrap interval. Report Bo1/Bo3 and with/without-line splits alongside the total. Also score the full schedule using the anchor wherever a candidate lacks data. For confidence, show correct picks, Points and coverage at each threshold: skipping a Match earns zero Points. Freeze the candidate and its thresholds before evaluating the reserved Holdout once. [Scoring](../../src/domain/scoring.rs) · [Protocol](../lab/research/report.md)
